# Vast replaces Expanded, and the New Game screen loses three dials

**Date:** 2026-09-16

Expanded's 25-point target could not be reached by building. [The
diagnosis](2026-09-16-expanded-endgame-supply-cliff.md) has the numbers: four
players claim only eight or nine vertices each on a 37-tile board, which caps a
player near 17 points of buildings, so the last points had to come from a 50-card
deck those same four players were emptying. Nineteen of forty games emptied it.

The fix is the quantity that was actually scarce - **room**.

## The board

| | classic | Expanded | **Vast** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| tiles | 19 | 37 | **61** |
| vertices | 54 | 96 | **150** |
| edges | 72 | 132 | **210** |
| target | 10 | 25 | **26** |

Composition: 3 deserts and 58 resource tiles, holding classic's 4:4:4:3:3 mix to
within a point so brick and ore stay the scarce pair the trading game is built
on. Tokens are symmetric about 7 exactly as the physical board is. 15 ports on a
54-edge coast (27.8%, against classic's ~30%).

Piece limits are sized to the vertices a player can claim rather than to area:
14 settlements, 12 cities, 38 roads, bank 60, deck 82 (46 knights, 16 victory
points, 7/7/6). Discard threshold 12.

### Why 26 and not 30

The target was derived, not chosen. Classic's 10 is about 109% of a player's max
building points - buildings get you most of the way and a bonus or a card
finishes it. Expanded's 25 is **147%** of its 17, which is the defect in one
number. On 61 tiles a player reaches ~13 vertices, so ~26 points of cities, and
26 restores classic's ratio. Two independent routes exist at that target, which
is the point: buildings plus a bonus, or buildings plus a couple of cards.
Thirty would have needed buildings *and* a bonus *and* cards - Expanded's failure
rebuilt one ring out.

## Measured, 24 seeded games, four Expert seats

| | Expanded (25) | **Vast (26)** |
|---|---:|---:|
| decisive | 39/40 | **24/24** |
| deck emptied | 19/40 | **0/24** |
| a piece cap reached | 24/40 | **0/24** |
| bank trades per turn | 0.49 (peak 3.29) | **0.34** |
| moves per game | 1,188 | **1,020** |
| ms per move | 71 | **66** |
| runner-up's share of target | 73% | **77%** |

A bigger board and a higher target, finishing *sooner* and closer. Both risks
predicted before building it were wrong, and measuring rather than assuming is
the only reason that is known:

- **Speed.** Predicted slower; it is faster, per move and per game. More room
  means fewer contested decisions to weigh.
- **Roads.** Predicted that the 38-road cap (`LongestRoad.supportedRoadLimit`,
  a measured performance cliff at 44, not a balance choice) would become the new
  bottleneck, since Expanded's bots finish on 23-28 of their 30. The most any bot
  used here was **30 of 38**. It never bound.

## What is deliberately still decodable

`GameMode.expanded` is retired from the New Game screen and **not deleted**.
Saves store the raw value, so removing the case makes `GameState.init(from:)`
throw on an in-progress Expanded game - and `GameStore.load()` swallows that with
`try?` and silently starts a new one. A player mid-match would simply lose it.
The case therefore stays playable and only leaves `GameMode.newGameChoices`.

The same reasoning applies to the two narrowings below: `supportedPlayerCounts`
stays `3...4` and `isValidMatch` still ignores the new-game target list, so a
three-player or 8-point save resumes exactly as it was.

## New Game screen, three dials removed (Jake, 2026-09-16)

- **Game Mode is a chip row**, not a popup. With two modes, a tap-to-open sheet
  was a second screen to reach a binary choice, and every other setting on the
  page was already a `PaintedChoiceRow`. `GameModePickerPopup` is deleted.
- **Table size is gone.** Four players only - "that's how these games are
  designed". The row hides itself while only one size is offered rather than
  rendering a single unusable chip, and a prefill saved at three seats is grown
  to four instead of being thrown away.
- **Match length is gone**, and is now a label. One target per mode: Classic 10,
  Vast 26. The 12-point option left with the three-player table, and it was
  always the fragile one - four-player 12-point self-play once reached
  11/11/11/10 and stayed there through 10,000 moves, the same supply-exhaustion
  defect that retired Expanded.
- **Seat 4's "Optional" pill is gone**, since there is no smaller table to drop
  it to.

Everything now fits on one screen without scrolling.

## Known rough edge

At the **first settlement placement** all 150 vertices are legal at once and
their highlights merge into a dense mesh. It clears immediately afterwards, when
the distance rule and road connectivity cull nearly all candidates, so only the
opening placement is affected. Ordinary play was photographed at real render size
and is legible - tokens readable, pieces clear, the whole board in frame.
